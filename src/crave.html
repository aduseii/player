Torrents play through WebTorrent, which connects to browser peers and web seeds. Torrents shared only by desktop clients (uTorrent, qBittorrent) won't find peers here. Streams from other sites play when the site allows it; screenshots of those need the site to allow cross-origin access. When a file's audio is Dolby or DTS, which Chrome and Firefox can't play, Crave converts it to AAC inside your browser. The converter downloads once (about 31 MB) and your file never leaves your computer.
